## Notes

- The ASGS Commonwealth Electoral Divisions are ABS approximations of the AEC's divisions built from
  Mesh Blocks. They are published by the ABS as ABS material and are not labelled as third-party
  content, and electorate names and postcodes are facts, so the "content supplied by third parties"
  exclusion does not apply (determination of cameron-young, 2026-10-12).
- No AEC boundary geometry (AEC Spatial Data Download Data Licence) is read to compile the data.
